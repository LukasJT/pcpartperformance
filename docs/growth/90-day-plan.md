# 90-day maintenance, editorial and distribution plan

Planning baseline: **October 13, 2026 (America/Toronto)**. Checkpoints are November 12, December 12 and January 11, 2027: 30, 60 and 90 days after the baseline. This is a capacity-based working plan, not an automation, publication schedule already activated or traffic forecast.

## Capacity and responsibilities

Assume **eight shared working hours per week**, with the owner available for one hour of real editorial/release decisions. Across thirteen weeks this is roughly 104 hours. If that capacity is unavailable, reduce the number of releases rather than weakening source checks.

| Weekly allocation | Hours | Responsible work |
|---|---:|---|
| Source/offer freshness and corrections | 2 | Check consequential model documentation, expired observations and broken references |
| One resource improvement or article research/revision | 2 | Improve a real tool journey or an existing matching article |
| Human editorial review and release decisions | 1 | Review complete content, sources and recommendation conditions; record truthful approval |
| Functional, visual and accessibility verification | 1 | Test the changed journey on phone/desktop, keyboard and both themes |
| Recording, editing or approved distribution | 1.5 | Prepare one small demonstration or advance a longer tutorial |
| Measurement and prioritization | 0.5 | Inspect available real metrics and annotate changes; no invented baseline |

A substantial guide may take several weeks. The practical content target is **up to six reviewed revisions over the period**, primarily from already prepared work, plus maintained tools and existing articles. This is neither six guaranteed publications nor fifty-six new URLs.

## Practical source-maintenance rules

- **Compatibility:** preserve exact board revision, CPU support entry, source dates, conditional slots and unknown installed BIOS. No broad socket-wide certification.
- **Prices:** a current exact seller/region/condition observation may expire. If a feed is not configured, use an honest manual quote/unknown state. No synthetic price history.
- **Performance:** record game/version/settings/scene/hardware/driver and redistribution rights. Requirements, mode targets, synthetic models and measurements remain distinct.
- **News:** retain source event versus actual publication dates. Follow up announced availability only when an original seller/manufacturer record or independent measurement supports it.
- **3D:** add only correctly identified usable licensed models and credit them. Geometry is an appearance aid unless a separately verified dimensional method supports a fit claim.
- **Editorial:** revise substance, sources or conditions before dateModified changes. An organizational byline does not imply invented human experts or site-owned tests.

## Measurement at 30/60/90 days

The current work has static-byte and functional evidence, not real-user growth or Core Web Vitals proof. Obtain authorized exports before filling traffic fields.

**Day 30 — November 12:** compare meaningful matched periods by country/device/landing-page cluster, separate branded/non-branded search, check indexing and source freshness, and validate actual event delivery if an adapter has been configured. Record impressions, clicks and completed tasks separately.

**Day 60 — December 12:** examine useful guide-to-tool journeys, search misses, supported versus unresolved checks and saved-plan usage. At low sample sizes, use recorded usability problems and cautious comparisons rather than claiming statistical lift.

**Day 90 — January 11:** review returning use, saved/shared-plan referrals and useful outbound actions where actually measured. Account for holiday seasonality, consent, browser/device identity and attribution limits. Keep AI impressions distinct from visits and external referrals.

Do not sum overlapping reports, label impressions unique visitors or attribute every change to one commit. Success for the plan is maintained evidence, usable connected journeys and measured learning, not a promised search position.
