## Current guidance applied

Use natural task language, exact entities and units. Do not stuff every phrase into headings or create articles for spelling variants. Original useful tools, complete answers, truthful attribution and sources serve people. [Google helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).

Keep category/product links in ordinary anchors and bound filter spaces. Review indexing before blocking already-indexed states: noindex requires readable crawling, canonical is not a crawl block, and robots is not authentication. [Faceted-navigation guidance](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/faceted-navigation).

Google does not require special AI schema, llms.txt, artificial tiny chunks or special phrasing. Natural entity relationships and useful accessible content are the basis; visibility is not guaranteed. [Generative-AI guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).
