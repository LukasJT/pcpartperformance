## Demonstration package to prepare after rendered verification

Six short demonstrations: choose a CPU and inspect remaining checks; skip a required part and see what remains; compare two exact GPUs; distinguish the four M.2 lengths from protocol support; record a reused PSU without assuming cable interchangeability; save a revision and restore it in My PC.

Three longer outlines: (1) choose → inspect → review → save a beginner build, (2) keep/replace/reuse with the complete platform-cost worksheet, (3) read a source-backed compatibility example without mistaking unknown for compatible. Show actual functioning screens only. Record no invented performance figures or false “live” prices.

Community-answer drafts should answer a concrete question first and link only where the tool adds utility. A proposed creator collaboration should name the relevant completed resource and why it helps that audience. Do not scrape private contact details or send/post anything without direct authorization. No fake community activity, purchased traffic, paid ranking links or automatic link drops.

## Refresh responsibilities

- Weekly: check errors, expired prices, broken consequential sources and newly reported data corrections; preserve old build data.

- Before provider activation: re-open official requirements, review current contracts/privacy details, confirm issued artifacts, test consent/CSP and ad placement, verify layout stability.
