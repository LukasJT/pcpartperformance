## Evidence and quality rubric

Each claim needs a route, source or executable receipt. Scores are not awarded from appearance alone.

- Navigation: no collisions or overflow across320/360/375/390/430/768/1024/1440/1920px; menu/keyboard/zoom recovery verified.
- Builder: three clear stages, preserved source controls, no automatic mobile keyboard, exact photos, focus return, optional skip semantics and restored saved records.
- Themes: shared layout, six palettes plus system/high contrast; tested readable text, focus, controls, status and charts; no load-time theme mismatch.
- Motion: shared timing, transform/opacity priority, offscreen/hidden-page suspension, manual pause, reduced-motion compliance; no essential content hidden without JavaScript.
- Visuals: actual optimized original files, useful category/technical meaning, correct alternatives, no asserted manufacturer photography or geometry certification.
- Editorial: unique intent, complete useful draft, source-linked consequential claims, truthful authorship/date/status, substantive review required before publication.
- SEO: intended canonical URLs, truthful schema, sitemap/robots consistency, no draft or internal-resource leakage, real link/status checks.
- Publisher readiness: current official evidence, actual observed configuration, owner-controlled dependencies explicit; no acceptance/eligibility assertion without provider/GA4 evidence.
- Performance: before/after comparable lab receipts, initial payload budgets and deferred3D; no field Core Web Vitals or ranking improvement inferred from lab results.
- Reliability: full existing checks plus changed-behavior tests; real rendered controls and save/restore journeys; no private browser/profile access.
