# CPU supplement photo review — 2026-10-17

17 exact photos accepted from a bounded review of 113 missing CPU IDs. 96 remain unresolved in this increment. All 52 candidate photos were inspected on contact sheets; accepted images were also inspected at source-preview size where needed.

All accepted photos come from Commons with file-specific author and license evidence. Credits and license URLs are retained in the registry. Each final image is derived from its recorded raw provider source bytes, with source-byte SHA-256, final SHA-256, thumbnail SHA-256 and measured sizes recorded. Eight use downloaded originals; nine use already cached licensed previews. sourceHashScope distinguishes these cases, imageUrl is the actual source used, and originalImageUrl is preserved separately. No further requests were made to rate-limited hosts. See the accepted contact sheets and per-ID audit.

## Limits

The audit distinguishes blocked requests, missing category URLs, generic or mismatched media, and candidates not established as exact. A missing Commons category is not a claim that no image exists. HTTP 429 batches were stopped, with backoff respected. No access-control bypass, private browser, generated photo, label replacement or unverified model alias was used.

Exact clock, architecture and package variants were preserved. In particular, AM2 Windsor 3800+ did not replace Manchester Socket 939; Deschutes 300 did not replace Klamath 300; 5 MHz 8085AH-2 and faster 8088-2 did not replace the catalog frequencies.

Existing storage/PSU and CPU-extra manifests were not modified. No build, commit or deployment was performed.
