# RTX 5070 vs RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB: memory, power and buying checks

URL: /compare/rtx-5070-vs-rtx-5060-ti-16gb/
Meta description: Compare RTX 5070 and RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB specifications, understand the VRAM tradeoff, and check exact card fit and benchmark settings before buying.
By PC Part Performance. Draft created October 3, 2026. First publication remains pending deployment.

The RTX 5070 has more CUDA cores but 12 GB of memory; the RTX 5060 Ti variant here has 16 GB and a lower reference power rating. Use game tests and the exact local listing to make the purchase decision.

## Manufacturer specifications

| Specification | rtx5070 | rtx5060ti16 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Video memory | 12 GB GDDR7 | 16 GB GDDR7 |
| CUDA cores | 6,144 | 4,608 |
| Maximum boost specification | 2.51 GHz | 2.57 GHz |
| Reference total graphics power | 250 W | 180 W |

## More memory and more rendering resources answer different questions

The extra capacity on the 16 GB card can matter when a workload needs it. It does not make memory capacity a ranking of GPU speed. Conversely, a higher core count is not a complete game-performance result. Compare the games, resolution and settings you intend to use.

Check the capacity in the retailer’s full model number. This page compares the 16 GB RTX 5060 Ti, not its 8 GB sibling. The table uses NVIDIA’s reference specifications; partner cards can differ in clocks, cooling and dimensions.

## Keep native FPS separate from generated frames

Use original game tests with the same settings for both cards. A frame-generation result and a native-rendering result answer different questions. Look for the underlying rendering rate, image quality and responsiveness as well as the displayed FPS counter.

The linked Tom’s Hardware test is independent original testing. We have not reproduced its measurements or converted its suite into a claimed result for every game. The site’s FPS calculator remains an estimate.

## Check the card you will actually order

An ASUS, MSI or other partner card is a physical product with its own cooler and connector layout. Read its dimensions and the case manual together, accounting for radiators and cable space. The ASUS cards shown here are examples of partner designs, not universal shapes for these GPUs.

Use the product links to check exact variants and retailers. A search result is not a checked offer. No current price is claimed on this page, so there is no price-based winner. Compare the final in-stock totals in CAD or USD when you are ready to buy.
