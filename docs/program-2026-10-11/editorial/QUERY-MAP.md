## Evidence basis

Inspected the prior 40 complete program drafts, their publication gate, the normal blog inventory and matching publication/AI/feature article sources before choosing the batch. Six qualitative searches on October 11 explored Canadian prebuilt requirements, US seller/SKU terms, DIY versus prebuilt costs, phone width versus screen diagonal, 3D preview clearance and regional price timestamps. Results showed relevant terminology and competing page types; they do not establish search volume, ranking difficulty, representative demand or promised traffic. Search Console data was not available to this work.

Every item has an exact route, natural title/description, explicit audience, original buyer analysis and a relevant tool handoff. No keyword-density target or artificial word-count ranking claim is used.

## Internal discovery

Preview links all 12 drafts from `/editorial-review/oct11/` and the four news drafts from `/news/`. Those review pages are noindex. Normal `/news/` links only eligible confirmed reports plus existing published background explainers. Draft article routes are removed from normal output/search and are not placed in the regular sitemap, RSS or news sitemap. The shared site integration must preserve those classifications after its navigation/SEO passes.

Original 1200×675 schematic PNGs and editable SVGs supply article-specific image context. Alt text describes the illustration. The artwork is not a product photo, benchmark chart or exact fit model. Headlines use the documented event or buyer question without fabricated tests, urgent-buy claims or a refreshed date.

## Follow-up measurement

After actual publication and verified indexing, the owner can use authorized Search Console data to inspect queries, impressions, clicks and competing site URLs. Those observations may justify revising a title, improving an existing URL or merging overlapping material. This work makes no claim that such access, measurements or rankings already exist.
