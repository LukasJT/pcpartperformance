# content freshness system

Prepared October 4, 2026. Local release; deployment confirmation is not recorded.

Research dates are preserved in publication metadata and source records. Stage 2 datePublished is null until confirmed hosting; source-check dates do not masquerade as publication. Evergreen modification dates change for material text/evidence edits. Existing Stage 1 dates were preserved.

Run scripts/audit-publication-freshness.cjs to compare source/content hashes against its previous audit and list review dates. It reports changes; it does not rewrite dates, publish a story or retitle it latest.

Quarterly evergreen check; monthly support/market policy check; event-driven release review. A scheduled cloud monitor is not active. Once a hosting source/automation list is verified, reuse its existing schedule or configure an owner-approved one. Intake is a draft signal, not autopublishing.
