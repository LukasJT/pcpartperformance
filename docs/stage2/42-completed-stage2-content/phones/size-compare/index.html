How the comparison works

The outlines use one common scale based on published body height and width. They represent rounded rectangles, not a photographic rendering or a verified camera-bump profile. The overlay compares their footprint; thickness and weight appear in the numerical result.

This display is not calibrated to show a phone at its actual physical size on your monitor. Browser zoom and screen density vary. It also does not measure usable display area, reachability or hand comfort. A case can change the dimensions and grip, so handle the intended model where possible.

Source and region limits

The five model pages link to Apple, Google and Samsung documentation. Regional connectivity, SIM arrangements and storage availability require separate verification. These older models form a focused comparison cohort, not a complete or newest-phone catalog.
