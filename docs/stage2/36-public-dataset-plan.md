# public dataset plan

Prepared October 4, 2026. Local release; deployment confirmation is not recorded.

Shipped site-authored aggregate category/field/image counts in hashed CSV/JSON, plus schema-versioned phone JSON. No full photo/model asset redistribution. CC0 applies only to authored aggregate counts, not third-party images, models or an entire copied catalog.

Report denominator=unique local IDs, snapshot date, formulas, hash and limitations. Counts are not representative of market share or demand. For future change reports, preserve both snapshots and disclose catalog additions that changed the denominator.

Retail time series and frame-time datasets wait for permitted sourcing and reproducible observations. Do not infer stock or benchmark samples from existing specification records.
