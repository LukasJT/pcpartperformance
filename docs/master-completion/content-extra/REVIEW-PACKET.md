## Important evidence boundaries

- **Terra:** the seven shared spine positions are the constraint. The worked dimensions are hypothetical arithmetic, explicitly described as such. No complete retail build or acoustic result is certified.
- **Original North:** 158 mm NH-U12A height is compared with 170 mm TG / Mesh-without-bracket and 145 mm Mesh-with-bracket limits. Only the corresponding narrow height check passes or fails. The LF III 240 bare radiator width does not establish full fan/fastener/tube and motherboard/RAM clearance.
- **Cooling:** AMD’s liquid-cooling recommendation and Noctua’s CPU-specific air support are both retained. No matched thermal or acoustic winner is invented.
- **Used parts:** a demonstration or diagnostic is a current observation, not a remaining-life guarantee. CORSAIR’s transfer exception and MSI’s regional context are not generalized to other manufacturers or interpreted as legal advice.
- **RTX 3060:** the cited candidate review does not contain a directly tested 3060 in its enumerated comparison set. Manufacturer charts with different frame-generation modes are not native-rendering uplift evidence. No price, FPS, latency or “best value” result is supplied.

The documentation guides are complete within those scopes. A **tested** quiet build, a matched complete-system cooler ranking and a current-price/matched-FPS GPU buying winner remain unsupported. [GAP-OVERLAY.json](GAP-OVERLAY.json) records that distinction instead of rewriting the pinned original gap map.

The six data-dependent briefs **P02–P05, P14 and P39** remain blocked individually. These guides do not create missing exact current offers, a complete regional BOM or permitted matched workload benchmarks.
