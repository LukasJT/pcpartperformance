## Public-search observations

Actual query set:

- `how to choose cpu cooler that fits RAM case radiator`
- `everything needed to build a PC cooler Windows hidden costs`
- `PCPartPicker compatibility notes CPU cooler RAM clearance Pangoly PC builder`
- `GPU front radiator clearance Fractal North exact case 300 355 mm`
- `cpu cooler ram clearance case height`
- `gaming pc build complete cost cooling operating system Canada`

The set surfaced manufacturer guides, component-specific clearance documentation, builders and community questions. Recurring distinctions include CPU socket versus full fit; taller RAM versus a raised front fan; a radiator’s nominal class versus its occupied dimensions; and whether a budget includes cooling, software and delivery. Those are useful language/intent observations, not quantified demand.

Implementation decisions:

1. Use ordinary question language in the cooler and GPU-fit titles. Do not reproduce misspellings or make prose unnatural to imitate a query.
2. Answer near the beginning, then provide an exact worked example and next action. A visitor should be able to apply the guide to the builder rather than restart research elsewhere.
3. Keep one general build-versus-buy cost URL and make its checklist more complete. Avoid additional Canada/US copies when the method is the same; keep region and currency explicit in the tools.
4. Keep diagrams explanatory. Their full meaning also exists in HTML paragraphs and an accessible table; image labels are not the only record of important facts.
5. Do not convert public-search observations into “popular,” “trending” or “most searched” claims.
