# 90-day maintenance and useful-content plan

Prepared October 16, 2026. **Day 1 begins after the verified release.** This is a capacity-based operating plan, not a scheduler configuration, promise of traffic or authorization to contact people. No outreach, newsletter, notification or social post is activated by this document.

This is the current operational summary for the October 16 implementation. Older October 9/13 planning documents are historical: their descriptions of inactive prices or unpublished older guide batches must not override current publication registries, daily observations and release receipts.

## Capacity and ownership

Assume **eight total working hours per week**, including one hour of owner/editor decisions. The thirteen-week window provides roughly 103 hours because the final week ends on Day 90. Staffing is an assumption, not a claim that people are assigned.

| Weekly allocation | Hours | Output |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Source/price health and corrections | 2 | Investigated freshness failures and accurate impact notes |
| One substantive content/research increment | 2.5 | A useful revision or progress on a distinct new guide |
| Engineering, accessibility and release QA | 1.5 | A tested fix or completed changed journey |
| Editorial/owner decisions | 1 | Source/recommendation review, truthful lifecycle and release decisions |
| Demonstration/distribution preparation and measurement | 1 | A draft asset or review of real available metrics |

A complex guide can span several weeks. A sensible ceiling is **up to six substantial article/resource revisions and up to two genuinely new guides**, six short feature demonstrations and one longer walkthrough across the quarter. These are capacity ceilings, not mandatory publications. If verification takes longer or an urgent correction appears, reduce output instead of weakening the evidence threshold.

After code/content changes, run the relevant checks and then the established release suite; do not repeatedly redeploy unchanged material solely to change dates.

## Publication and distribution controls

For each proposed article, find the existing matching intent first. Keep the answer near the beginning, attach sources to consequential claims, disclose assumptions at the decision point and connect to a useful working tool. Preserve first publication dates; record a material update when substance changes. Do not claim a human review, original test, fresh quote or shipped feature that did not occur.

The existing guides are a foundation, not a command to republish every historical draft. Complete BOM/budget recommendations require actual regional offers or clearly user-entered quotes. Performance recommendations need applicable test evidence and rights. Distinct original diagrams and worksheets can be useful without copying competitor photos, layouts or benchmark tables.

Prepare demonstrations from the actual verified live pages. Avoid showing a fictional note, sample fit calculation or illustrative build as a visitor testimonial, real checkout or measured result. Existing video/community/outreach drafts are reusable starting material, but recheck factual and release-status claims before use.

Potential audiences: first-time-build educators who can use a printable readiness checklist; hardware creators explaining exact clearance; and existing-PC owners comparing whole-platform spending. A collaboration draft should say which completed resource helps that audience. Do not scrape private contact information, send messages, purchase links, seed fake recommendations or automate forum posting. The owner must authorize the actual destination/action before outreach.

## Measurement checkpoints

No analytics destination or Search Console property is connected by this plan. The existing `PCP_EVENTS` gate remains default-off until a real approved adapter, consent state and delivery QA exist. Do not add a second tracker or collect raw queries, free-form notes, private IDs, full shared configurations or warranty/serial text.

If access becomes available:

- **Day 30:** validate event delivery and denominators first. Separate search impressions from clicks, arriving users from completed tasks and selected shopping country from observed visitor geography.
- **Day 60:** examine meaningful build/save/restore and upgrade handoffs, guide-to-tool arrivals and unsuccessful searches. Inspect actual sessions/usability evidence before changing wording solely for a tiny CTR fluctuation.
- **Day 90:** compare non-branded acquisition, referrals and returning use over comparable periods, with country/device/content-cluster differences and seasonality. Report consent/device/identity limitations. Do not equate cookies or summed daily users with an exact unique-person count.

Maintain a release annotation containing commit, date, actual affected routes and content revisions. Report traffic, utility, data coverage and errors separately. A passed test, Article schema, sitemap submission, useful animation or improved payload is not proof of ranking, Google News placement, additional visitors or income.

## Quarterly decision

Continue the improvements that remove a documented user problem or increase verified evidence coverage. Defer attractive but unsupported services. The next highest-value unresolved dependencies are broader reproducible retailer coverage, permitted measured-PC benchmark data, additional accurately identified/licensed models, and authorized measurement. Neither this schedule nor a new interface makes those dependencies complete.
